## Doctor Credentials

### Dr. William P. O'Gara (DDS)

- Born and raised in Reno, Nevada; graduated from Bishop Manogue Catholic High School; Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of Nevada, graduating Summa Cum Laude.
- Attended the top nationally-ranked Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, University of the Pacific, San Francisco; awarded the Gold Foil Award for Clinical Excellence and graduated top of his class.
- Completed the University of Louisville orthodontic residency program; returned as a distinguished alumni lecturer.
- Founded O'Gara Orthodontics in 1990.
- Accomplishments: Delegate for the Young Dentists of America; Hands on Herbst appliance training; Appointed Delegate for Nevada Dental Association; Appointed by Governor Sandoval to the Nevada State Board of Dental Examiners; President of Northern Nevada Dental Association; Recipient of the RGJ Best Orthodontist in Reno 2021; McLaughlin straight wire training; Dentaurum temporary anchorage device training; Graduate of Fort Knox Advanced Orthodontic Education Program; Member of National Dental Honor Society, Omicron Kappa Upsilon; Member of the American Dental Association; Member of the Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontics; Member of the American Association of Orthodontists; Member of the Nevada Dental Association; Member of the Northern Nevada Dental Association.
- In February 2021, welcomed Dr. K.C. Gilbert as a partner, forming O'Gara-Gilbert Orthodontics.

### Dr. K.C. Gilbert (DMD, MS)

- Nevada native born and raised in Reno; graduated from Bishop Manogue High School; degree in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Nevada, Reno.
- Dental school at Oregon Health and Science University (OHSU): served as OHSU Student Body President, OHSU School of Dentistry Vice President, and president of his dental school class; finished in the top 5% of his class.
- Accolades: Induction into the National Dental Honor Society, Omicron Kappa Upsilon; Recipient of the faculty nominated Pierre Fauchard Foundation Dental Student Scholarship Award; Recipient of the MODA/Oregon Dental Society Scholarship; Recipient of the OHSU Dental School Leadership award; Recipient of the OHSU Dental School Biology Award; Recipient of the OCTRI Summer Fellowship Award for Research; Primary author of a published textbook chapter in "Epidemiology – Theory, Research and Practice"; Co-primary author of research presented at the Oregon Dental Conference and the International Association of Dental Research general session.
- Trained at the University of Florida Orthodontics residency program: earned a Certificate in Orthodontics and a Master of Science in Dental Sciences; served as Chief Resident in his third year.
- Involved in organized dentistry in Nevada, holding positions in both the Nevada Dental Association and the Northern Nevada Dental Association.

### Dr. John Silvaroli (DMD, MS)

- Reno resident since 1997; Galena High School graduate; undergraduate work at the University of Nevada, Reno.
- Dental education at UNLV School of Dental Medicine, graduating Magna Cum Laude and at the top of his class; president of the SGT Ferrin Veteran's Memorial Clinic; conducted oral cancer research; presented as the keynote oral presenter for Nutritional Sciences at the International Association of Dental Research in Seoul, Korea.
- Achievements: Authored two journal publications; Inducted into the National Dental Honor Society, Omicron Kappa Upsilon; Awarded the OKU William S. Kramer Award of Excellence; Recipient of the award for the Development of the Oro-Facial Complex by the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO).
- Completed orthodontic specialization at the University of Oklahoma; defended thesis "The Incidence of Parafunctional Habits in Association with Clear Aligner Therapy" and received his Masters of Science.
